Potato Chips

Description

Potato chips (or crisps) are thin slices of potato, fried or baked until crunchy, then salted and/or seasoned, known for their crispy texture, salty taste, and satisfying crunch, making them a globally popular snack served plain, with flavors like cheese & onion, or as a versatile side.

Ingredient

  • Potatoes
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Onion Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Spices
  • Citric Acid
  • Sodium Diacetate
  • Spice Extractives

Steps

  1. Prepare Potatoes
  2. Soak
  3. Dry Completely
  4. Heat Oil
  5. Fry in Batches
  6. Drain & Season
  7. Cool & Serve
