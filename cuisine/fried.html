Fried Rice

Description

Fried rice is a dish of cooked rice stir-fried in a pan, typically with other ingredients like scrambled eggs, vegetables, and a protein such as chicken, beef, or shrimp. It is a popular Asian dish, originating from China, that can be customized with various sauces like soy sauce and seasonings, and is often made with leftover, day-old rice for the best texture.

Ingredient

  • 1 cup Coles Long Grain White Rice.
  • 2 tsp vegetable oil.
  • 2 tsp vegetable oil.
  • 2 eggs, lightly whisked.
  • 2 bacon rashers, chopped.
  • 1 carrot, peeled and grated.
  • 2 shallots, trimmed, finely sliced.
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas, thawed.
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce.

Steps

  1. Prepare Ingredients: Ensure all ingredients are chopped into uniform, bite-size pieces and ready before you start cooking, as the process is very fast.
  2. Heat oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook any raw protein (like chicken or shrimp) until done and set it aside.
  4. Add your aromatics (garlic, onions, ginger) and stir-fry until fragrant
  5. Add vegetables (like carrots and peas) and stir-fry until tender but still crisp, about 2-4 minutes.
  6. Add the cold, cooked rice to the pan, breaking up any clumps with a spatula or your hands beforehand.
